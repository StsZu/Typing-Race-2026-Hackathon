01 / THE BASICS

Eyes on the text.
Fingers know the way.

Touch typing means typing with all ten fingers without looking at the keyboard. Instead of hunting for each letter, you gradually memorize the movement.

Find your home position

Press a key on the diagram to see which finger types it.

F and J: your index fingers. Feel for the bumps on these keys.

① Little finger② Ring finger③ Middle finger④ Index finger

Ukrainian ЙЦУКЕН. Home row: Ф І В А · О Л Д Ж. Ґ may need a separate key or a key combination, depending on your OS.

01

Bring your fingers home

Left hand on A S D F, right hand on J K L ;. Your thumbs take care of the space bar.

02

Keep your hands relaxed

Relax your shoulders, keep your wrists straight and don’t rest them on the edge of the desk. The diagram shows finger zones, not the shape of a particular keyboard.

03

Don’t rush

The right finger and accuracy come first. If you make many mistakes, slow down. Instead of glancing down, bring your attention back to the text.

06 / FROM FIRST TRY TO HABIT

30 days. At your own pace.

Set aside 10–15 minutes a day. Take breaks and come back to the difficult keys. A specific speed after 30 days is not guaranteed.

DAYS 01–07

Getting started

Finger placement and the home row. Slow drills without looking down.

DAYS 08–14

Accuracy

Top and bottom rows, numbers, Shift. Add keys gradually.

DAYS 15–21

Rhythm

Short words and common letter pairs. A steady pace and working on mistakes.

DAYS 22–30

Confidence

Emails, notes, real texts. Compare your result with day one.
