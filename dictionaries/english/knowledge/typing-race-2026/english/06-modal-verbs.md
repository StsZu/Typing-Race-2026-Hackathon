Modal verbs add meaning to the main verb and are followed by the base form. We use can for ability and permission, as in I can swim and Can I sit here. We use must and have to for obligation, for example You must stop at a red light. We use should for advice, as in You should rest. We use may and might for possibility, such as It might snow later. Modals do not change with he, she, or it, and they do not take do in questions.
