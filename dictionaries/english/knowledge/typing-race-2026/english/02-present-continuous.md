We use the present continuous for actions that are happening now or around the present time. We form it with the verb to be and a verb ending in ing, for example I am reading a book and they are working today. It also describes temporary situations and future arrangements, as in She is staying with friends this week. Words like now, at the moment, and currently are common signals. Some verbs, such as know and like, are not usually used in this form.
