We use the present perfect to connect the past with the present. We form it with have or has and the past participle, for example I have finished my work and she has visited London. It describes experiences, recent actions, and situations that are still true, as in They have lived here for ten years. Words such as already, yet, just, ever, and never are common. We do not use it with a finished time like yesterday; there we use the past simple.
