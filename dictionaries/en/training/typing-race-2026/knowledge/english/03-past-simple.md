We use the past simple for finished actions and events at a definite time in the past. Regular verbs end in ed, for example I watched a film and they visited Kyiv last year. Many common verbs are irregular, such as go which becomes went and see which becomes saw. We make questions and negatives with did, as in Did you call her and I did not know. Time expressions like yesterday, last week, and in twenty twenty often go with this tense.
