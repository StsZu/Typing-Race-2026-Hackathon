We use the present simple to talk about habits, routines, and things that are generally true. For example, I work in an office, she drinks coffee every morning, and the sun rises in the east. With he, she, and it we add an s to the verb. We make questions and negatives with do or does, as in Do you like tea and He does not eat meat. Time words such as always, usually, and every day often appear with this tense.
